    How this is worked out. DA is calculated on basic pay only, excluding personal pay, and each month's figure is rounded to the nearest rupee. For every month the DA due at the revised rate is compared with the 14.07% already drawn, and the difference is the arrear for that month. From September 2026 the full 19.27% comes in the pay bill, so arrears stop at August 2026. Special pay, family planning incentive increments and personal pay granted as advance increments are not reckoned for DA.
